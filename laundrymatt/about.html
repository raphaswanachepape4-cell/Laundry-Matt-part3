We are a team of professionals dedicated to providing the best laundry services to our customers. We are based in Midrand, Kempton park and Pretoria. Not only that but we do deliver to several surrounding areas such as Benoni, Johannesburg and many more.

Laundry‑Matt was born out of a simple but pressing need in Waterfall, Midrand: busy professionals, families, and businesses were struggling to keep up with laundry demands while balancing their daily responsibilities. Founded in 2023, the company quickly positioned itself as more than just a laundry service — it became a convenience solution. By offering affordable prices, free delivery, and specialized care for both individual and corporate clients, Laundry‑Matt began filling a gap in the market where traditional laundromats often fell short. The founders envisioned a service that combined reliability with accessibility, ensuring that customers could trust their garments would be handled with care while saving them valuable time. With ambitions to expand across all nine provinces of South Africa, Laundry‑Matt set out to build a strong digital presence to match its growing physical footprint. The website proposal reflects this vision: creating a platform that not only informs but also engages customers, showcasing branch details, service packages, and the company’s mission to become the nation’s leading laundry and dry‑cleaning provider.
